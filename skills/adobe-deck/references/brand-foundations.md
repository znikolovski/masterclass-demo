# Adobe Deck — Brand Foundations

## The Vision

Adobe presentations must be:
- **Succinct:** only what is absolutely needed
- **Clean:** simple orderly grids, lots of negative space
- **Consistent:** any slide should slot into any other Adobe deck and feel like the same hand made it
- **Relevant:** custom-tailored for the target audience
- **Professional yet playful:** kind, respectful, confident, smart, with a fun side
- **Accessible:** legible for people with visual or auditory impairment
- **On-brand:** Adobe Clean font always, Adobe icons always, Adobe Stock/Firefly imagery always, red thread and wordmark on every slide

## Colour Palette

### Primary palette
| Role | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Black | `#000000` | Text on white backgrounds; dark slide backgrounds |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Text on black backgrounds; primary content slide background |
| Adobe Red | `#EB1000` | Title slides, closer slides, red thread, wordmark. Sparingly on content slides. |

All text must be black on white or white on black. No coloured body text. No coloured backgrounds for content — colour comes from imagery.

### Extended palette (charts and diagrams only)
| Hex | Colour |
|-----|--------|
| `#FFA311` | Amber |
| `#FF66CC` | Pink |
| `#3A63F9` | Blue |
| `#0AA35B` | Green |
| `#F3C600` | Yellow |
| `#949494` | Mid gray (lightest on white) |
| `#616161` | Dark gray (darkest on black) |

Extended palette is accent highlight only — not for backgrounds, section colour-coding, or general design elements. Never use tints of any palette colour.

### Red rules
- `#EB1000` only. Never `#FF0000` or any substitute. Never tints of red.
- Never use red in financial charts — use gray, blue, green instead.
- In process/workflow diagrams red is acceptable but can be swapped for any extended palette colour.
- Big splashes of colour come from photography, illustrations, and screenshots — not from solid colour blocks.

## Typography

### Fonts
| Weight | Use |
|--------|-----|
| Adobe Clean Display Black | Headlines, slide titles |
| Adobe Clean Bold | Emphasis within body copy |
| Adobe Clean Regular | All body copy, descriptor text under stats |
| Adobe Clean (fallbacks) | Arial Black for Display Black; Arial for Regular/Bold |

Adobe Clean is for Adobe communications only and is not available for general public use. If not installed, use the fallbacks.

### Size scale
| Element | Size | Weight |
|---------|------|--------|
| Slide title | 28–36pt | Display Black |
| Speaker name/title on title slide | 20pt | Display Black |
| Section header / column header | 18–24pt | Display Black or Bold |
| Body / bullet text | 16–22pt | Regular |
| Stat number | 60–72pt | Display Black |
| Stat descriptor | ~1/3 of stat size | Regular |
| Stat unit (M, B, %) | ~80% of stat size | Regular (not subscript) |
| Currency symbols in stats | same as descriptor, superscript | Regular |
| Captions, footnotes | 10–12pt | Regular |

### Hierarchy rule (bullets)
- Bullet level 1: 22pt Regular
- Bullet level 2: 20pt Regular
- Bullet level 3: 18pt Regular (avoid where possible)

## Text Formatting

- **Case:** Sentence case on all slide titles. First word + proper nouns only. No title case.
- **Justification:** Left-align all body text and multi-line blocks. Centre only single-line image or chart titles. Right-align only single lines in specific diagram contexts (e.g. timelines). Never full-justify.
- **Pipe separator:** `Core concept  |  Sub concept` — 2 spaces each side. Used in slide titles and speaker name + title formatting.
- **No accent/decorative lines under titles.** Use whitespace instead.
- **No decorative full-width coloured bars, header ribbons, or footer stripes.**
- **Stats formatting:** Round as much as possible. Shorten millions to M, billions to B. Currency symbols superscript. Tilde and currency symbols same size as descriptor (not stat), superscript. Unit sizing rules apply only to standalone stats, not inline body copy.

## The Red Thread

- A design element that appears on every content slide, tying to Adobe's corporate style.
- **Never break** the red thread with text or images.
- The red thread can go over the top of a full-bleed image (use the designated slide layout).
- **Never recolour or modify** the red thread.
- Keep content inside the content area to give the red thread appropriate padding.

---

## Grid and Content Area

- The content area sits between defined guides: Header Area / Content Area / Footer Area.
- **Keep all content within the content area on content slides.** Do not break the guides except on full-bleed or half-bleed layouts specifically designed for it.
- Division markers split the content area into equal segments (halves, thirds, quarters).
- Guides: turn on with Alt+F9 (Windows) or Cmd+Opt+Ctrl+G (Mac).
- Reset button (Home tab, Slides group) resets placeholders to the correct size and position when migrating legacy content.
- Minimum margins: 0.5" from slide edges.
- Between content blocks: 0.3–0.5" consistently.
- Columns are denoted by whitespace — never by divider lines.
- Leave breathing room — the content area is intentionally smaller in the current template to encourage focus and give the wordmark and red thread space.

---

## White Space and Clarity

- White space directs the eye. Never fill every inch of a slide.
- Presented version: max 5 bullets × 6–7 words. Reduce content, not font size.
- Every presented slide readable in under 10 seconds.
- Handout version can carry more detail; avoid 3rd-level bullets.
- Text-heavy presentations: white backgrounds. Image-heavy presentations: dark backgrounds (where Rebecca's light preference allows).
