    Insert formats

    Templates (JSON) List of { "name", "template" }. Placeholders: {{sku}} {{name}} {{price}} {{url}} {{urlKey}} {{image}}. Plain text is typed at the cursor; HTML is inserted, and AEM block markup (<div class="block-name"><div><div>…) becomes a block table. Site-wide formats come from a "commerce-templates" tab (name, template) in the site config.
